# Diagram 0: the hero illustration

A hero image in the style of a product teardown sketch: the device drawn as
a clean, semi-transparent technical rendering in the middle, surrounded by
hand-drawn sketches on graph paper, each connected by a thin leader line to
a handwritten label.

**Centre:** the lumo-watch, face-on, slightly larger than life: a 1.75" round
AMOLED in a square module, shown as a see-through technical drawing (thin dark
outlines, light fills, a few orange accents) so the parts behind the glass
are visible: the ESP32-S3 module, the ribbon to the panel, the USB-C port on
one edge, the two buttons (PWR and BOOT) on the side, the TF-card slot, the
small battery connector. On the screen, the GMT face: dark dial, a blue/red
two-tone 24-hour bezel, gold hands, a gliding second hand mid-sweep. One
subtle orange arc around the bezel with an arrowhead, suggesting it turns. No
brand names anywhere; the dial text reads "ZEPHYR".

**Four sketched callouts in the corners, pencil-and-marker style, loose and
slightly scribbled, with faint handwritten notes around them:**

- Top left, label **"Hardware"**: a green PCB fragment with the ESP32-S3
  module on it, a few passives, and a round display panel leaning against it.
- Top right, label **"Two cores"**: two small cartoon chips side by side; one
  holds a tiny clock and a USB plug, the other holds a paintbrush and a
  screen; a short zigzag "mailbox" line between them with an envelope on it.
- Bottom left, label **"Trace"**: an oscilloscope-like screen showing a
  timeline with coloured bars (pink, blue, red) and a vertical playhead, a
  small cable running from it toward the watch.
- Bottom right, label **"Volume knob"**: a laptop with a volume overlay on its
  screen, and the watch's rainbow-bar knob ring next to it with a curved
  arrow; a USB cable loops between them.

**Style:** white graph-paper background with a faint grid; the central device
is precise and vector-like; the corner sketches are hand-drawn, with pencil
shading and a little colour (green board, orange accents, rainbow ring).
Leader lines are thin, dark, with small circles at both ends. Labels in a
handwritten marker font. Wide landscape, about 2:1, suitable as a page header.
No logos, no brand names, no mascots.
